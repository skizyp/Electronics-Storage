# November 10, 2026: Made a 2d sketch.

Today I made a sketch of the storage box. It's not perfect yet, but it gives me a good idea of how everything will fit. The design has 2 bigger drawers and 4 smaller ones.

I also came up with an idea to add raised edges around the top, so I can put bottles or other equipment on it without them falling off.
